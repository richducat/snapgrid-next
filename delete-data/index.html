Snapgrid Remix

Delete Snapgrid Data

Ranked Snapgrid play creates a wallet-based player profile. Offline Practice and Remix media can stay entirely on your device.

Disconnect your wallet

Open Snapgrid Remix, tap the wallet button to open Player Profile, and tap Sign Out. This removes the private session token from the device and revokes that session on the Snapgrid server.

Remove Remix Kit media

Open Remix Kits and tap Reset. This removes the active kit's saved references to its selected photo and six optional sounds. Delete each additional kit or clear app storage to remove every kit at once.

Delete all local app data

On Android, open Settings → Apps → Snapgrid Remix → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This deletes local practice data, themes, Remix Kits, settings, media references, pending payment recovery data, and the local sign-in session. It does not delete your online player record.
