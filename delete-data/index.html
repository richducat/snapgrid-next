Snapgrid Remix

Delete Snapgrid Data

Snapgrid Remix does not create a developer-hosted player account or gameplay profile. Optional USDC purchases do create limited payment fulfillment records as described below.

Disconnect your wallet

Open Snapgrid Remix, tap the wallet button to open Player Profile, and tap Sign Out. Wallet authorization and the connected public address are held in memory only and are also cleared when the app process ends.

Remove Remix Kit media

Open Remix Kits and tap Reset. This removes the active kit's saved references to its selected photo and six optional sounds. Delete each additional kit or clear app storage to remove every kit at once.

Delete all local app data

On Android, open Settings → Apps → Snapgrid Remix → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This deletes scores, game tokens, level and daily progress, power-ups, themes, Remix Kits, settings, and media references stored by the app. Uninstalling Snapgrid Remix also removes its local data.

Payment and blockchain records

If you made a USDC purchase, Snapgrid keeps a limited fulfillment record containing the public wallet, product, amount, transaction signature, and delivery time. Public Solana transactions also remain visible on the blockchain. Clearing or uninstalling the app cannot remove blockchain data.
