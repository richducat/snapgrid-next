Seeker Remix Rush Rules

Planned campaign: August 10, 2026 at 16:00 UTC through August 31, 2026 at 16:00 UTC

No purchase or paid entry is required. This is a skill-based game campaign, not a random drawing, wager, token sale, investment offer, or promise of cash or transferable crypto.

1. Sponsor and acceptance

Seeker Remix Rush is offered by Richard Ducat / EB28 through Snapgrid Remix. By entering, you accept these rules and the Snapgrid Terms and Privacy Policy. These planned dates become final only when the 1.2 update and campaign are announced live; any approved change will be posted here before play begins.

2. Eligibility

The global Daily Challenge is available to eligible Snapgrid players with a verified Solana wallet. The Seeker leaderboard and Founder Pack require a wallet holding a valid Seeker Genesis Token. Participants must be at least 18 or the age of legal majority where they live, must not be subject to applicable sanctions, and must not participate where this campaign is prohibited. Sponsor staff and anyone administering results may play but are not eligible for weekly rewards.

3. Campaign periods

  • Campaign: August 10, 2026 16:00 UTC to August 31, 2026 16:00 UTC.
  • Week 1: August 10 16:00 UTC to August 17 16:00 UTC.
  • Week 2: August 17 16:00 UTC to August 24 16:00 UTC.
  • Week 3: August 24 16:00 UTC to August 31 16:00 UTC.
  • A Daily Challenge follows the UTC date returned by the Snapgrid server.

4. Free Daily Challenge

Each player receives one free Daily Challenge attempt per UTC day. All players receive the same server-generated board seed. Power-ups are disabled. The server replays the submitted move log and uses only the replayed score, line clears, combo, moves, and completion time. A client-reported score cannot create a leaderboard result. A result must arrive before the run ticket, UTC day, campaign week, and campaign itself close; the earliest of those times is shown by the game server.

5. Ranking and ties

Daily entries rank by highest verified score, then fewer verified moves, then earliest verified completion. If all three values are identical, wallet byte order provides a deterministic final order. Weekly Seeker rank uses each player's best verified result for each UTC day in that week, sums those scores and moves, and applies the same score, moves, then completion-time order. The server record is final except for a documented correction of a material technical error.

6. Seeker verification and Founder Pack

Snapgrid verifies that the connected wallet owns a valid Seeker Genesis Token, verifies wallet ownership by signed message, and records the Genesis Token mint. Each mint may claim once, even if it later moves to a different wallet. The first 5,000 valid mints claimed before campaign end receive 500 promotional RMX, the Neon Seeker board, Seeker sound set, animated Founder badge, and Seeker leaderboard access. The maximum Founder Pack issuance is 2,500,000 promotional RMX.

8. Campaign unlock

Completing seven different Daily Challenges during the campaign unlocks the campaign-only win animation. A completion must be accepted by server replay. The unlock has no score or reward multiplier.

9. Prohibited conduct

Bots, scripts, macros, emulators used for automation, modified clients, clock manipulation, replay or score tampering, forged Seeker metadata, wallet or mint cycling, duplicate claims, collusion, abuse of outages, ad or payment fraud, denial-of-service activity, and exploitation of an undisclosed bug are prohibited. Snapgrid may request reasonable verification, reject a result, remove an entry, withhold an affected reward, or disqualify a player. Good-faith bug reports sent before exploitation will be reviewed separately.

10. Outages and changes

If a material server, Solana, wallet, or campaign error prevents fair play, Snapgrid may pause the campaign, extend an affected period, rerun a challenge, or void only the affected results. Any material change will be posted on this page and, when practical, in the app. Snapgrid will not substitute a random winner.

11. Privacy and publicity

Public leaderboards show a shortened public wallet address, score, moves, rank, and Seeker status. Snapgrid processes the records described in its Privacy Policy. Aggregate, non-identifying campaign results may be shared with Solana Mobile and the public. No real name or contact information is required for leaderboard play.

12. Relationship to Solana Mobile

Solana Mobile may choose to promote the campaign but is not the sponsor, administrator, or prize provider. Solana and Seeker names are used to describe compatible technology and eligibility.
