Snapgrid Remix

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 14, 2026

Snapgrid Remix is a puzzle game from Richard Ducat / EB28. This policy explains the data used by the Android Solana edition, its online game service, the iOS edition, and the website.

Practice and local Remix data

Offline Practice stores settings, a local practice best, board theme, and Remix Kit choices on your device. A Remix Kit may reference a photo and short sounds selected through Android's system picker. Snapgrid does not upload those photos or sounds. Private app storage may also temporarily hold a pending public payment signature or a signed-in session token so delivery and sign-in can recover safely. On Android, the session token is encrypted with a key held by Android Keystore.

Wallet sign-in

Ranked play uses Mobile Wallet Adapter and Sign In With Solana. Snapgrid receives a public wallet address, a signed human-readable message, its signature, and an optional wallet account label. The signature proves control of the address and cannot move funds. Snapgrid never receives a seed phrase or private key. A hashed session identifier, rather than the raw session token, is stored on the server.

Online player and game records

The game service stores the public wallet address; RMX ledger entries and balance buckets; XP, level, daily progress, streaks, inventory, cosmetics, pass status, and campaign unlocks; run seeds, start and completion times, move logs, independently replayed results, and anti-cheat outcomes; and leaderboard entries. Public leaderboards associate a score, move count, time, and Seeker status with a public wallet address. The app shortens the address for display.

Seeker verification

If you request Seeker verification, Snapgrid checks public Token-2022 accounts and exact Seeker Genesis Token metadata using a private Solana RPC provider. It stores the verified Genesis Token mint, wallet, verification time, and claim time. The mint is retained as a one-Token claim guard even if the token later moves to a different wallet.

USDC purchases

For an optional purchase, the service processes the public wallet, product, amount, USDC accounts, order reference, public transaction signature, final status, fulfillment identifier, and time. These records prevent duplicate delivery, support accounting, and help resolve failed fulfillment. Public Solana transactions remain visible on the blockchain independently of Snapgrid.

Rewarded ads and privacy choices

The Android edition uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for consent and privacy choices. Google may process device, advertising, diagnostics, consent, and interaction data under its policies. Snapgrid sends a short-lived signed claim identifier as server-side verification data. Snapgrid credits RMX only after validating Google's signed callback and stores the transaction ID to prevent duplicate rewards. You can reopen ad privacy choices from the Shop.

Service and security data

Hosting, Solana RPC, wallet, and ad providers receive ordinary network data such as IP address, device or app information, and request timing. Snapgrid uses temporary rate-limit data, security logs, and error data to protect accounts, payments, campaigns, and leaderboards. Snapgrid does not use wallet addresses for cross-app advertising profiles and does not sell personal data.

Retention

Expired sign-in challenges and sessions are removed on an operational schedule. Player, immutable ledger, payment, anti-fraud, Seeker claim, campaign, and leaderboard records are retained as reasonably needed for gameplay, security, accounting, dispute handling, campaign integrity, and legal obligations. Aggregate, non-identifying campaign reports may be retained longer. Encrypted application and hosting-provider database backups follow the operational retention schedule. Data deleted from the live service may remain in a protected backup until that backup expires or is securely replaced, and is not restored except for recovery.

iOS and website

The iOS edition may use Apple Game Center, StoreKit, AdServices, Google AdMob, and the User Messaging Platform. The website processes ordinary hosting requests. Those providers process data under their own policies.

Your choices and deletion

You may use Offline Practice without signing in, decline purchases and ads, sign out, remove Remix media, or clear Android app storage. To request access, correction, or deletion of eligible server-held data, see the data deletion page or email us. Public blockchain data cannot be deleted, and some payment, immutable ledger, security, and one-Token claim records may be retained where necessary for fraud prevention, accounting, campaign integrity, or law.

Children

Snapgrid is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. Seeker Remix Rush and wallet purchases are limited by their published eligibility rules and applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate permission.

Security and changes

Snapgrid uses signed wallet challenges, hashed sessions, HTTPS, server replay, idempotent writes, an append-only ledger, and least-privilege services. No system is perfectly secure. We may update this policy and will post the new effective date here.
